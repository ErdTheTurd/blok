Features

Everything Blok removes, and everything it labels.

Blok does two jobs. It blocks ads and trackers for free, and with Pro it marks writing, images and videos that were probably made by AI.

Ad blocking · Free

Ads never load in the first place.

Blocking happens in the browser's own network layer, before a page can download an ad. Pages get lighter, not heavier.

Ads and trackers, blocked by the browser itself.

Blok uses the same filter lists as uBlock Origin: EasyList for ads and EasyPrivacy for trackers, over 100,000 rules in total. Blok hands them to Safari, Chrome or Edge, which apply them natively. That's faster and easier on the battery than a blocker that inspects every page in JavaScript.

Every kind of YouTube ad.

YouTube sends ads inside the same data as the video. Blok removes them from that data before the player reads it, so there's no ad to wait out and no black screen.

It also hides the ads YouTube puts in the home feed, search results, Shorts and the merch shelf under videos. If an ad still gets through after a YouTube update, Blok mutes it and skips to the end.

Sites don't see it coming.

Many sites check whether their ads loaded and show a wall if they didn't. Blok hides elements in a layer the page can't inspect, and it answers the common detection scripts the way they expect, so the wall never appears.

If a site still breaks, pause Blok for that site from the toolbar button. It reloads without Blok, and Blok remembers your choice.

AI labels · Pro

Not a guess. A score you can check.

Blok combines several independent signals into one percentage and shows it next to the content. It gets more accurate every time someone taps 👍 or 👎.

  1. 1

    Labels the AI company added

    Under the EU AI Act and similar rules, more AI tools now tag what they make. Blok checks Content Credentials (C2PA), IPTC "AI-generated" metadata and YouTube's altered-or-synthetic disclosure first. When a tag is there, Blok is near-certain.

  2. 2

    Analysis on your device

    Most AI text isn't tagged, so Blok looks at phrasing, structure and rhythm, right on your device. Nothing you read is uploaded. On Apple devices with Apple Intelligence, Pro can run a second, deeper check.

  3. 3

    Votes from other people

    Every 👍 and 👎 is sent as an anonymous fingerprint of the content. When enough people agree about the same post, image or video, everyone's label for it gets more accurate.

Images and videos too.

Images from Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, Google and others often carry a signature saying they were generated. Blok reads it and labels the image, even when the page doesn't mention it.

On YouTube, Blok reads the uploader's "altered or synthetic content" disclosure, looks at the title and description, and adds what other viewers voted.

  • Image signed by Adobe FireflyContent Credentials
  • Video marked synthetic by uploaderYouTube disclosure

You decide what happens next.

Labels never remove anything. Hide blurs the post until you tap Show. Thumbs down removes the label and teaches Blok to label less like it for you. Thumbs up confirms it for everyone.

Labels appear at 70% and up by default. Set sensitivity to Low for fewer labels or High to catch more.
