What HaltBlock is

HaltBlock blocks ads and trackers in your browser, cleans up YouTube ads, and — with HaltBlock Pro — labels AI-generated text, images and YouTube videos, and can skip sponsor reads, self-promo and subscribe reminders on YouTube via SponsorBlock. Ad blocking is free. Pro features require a purchase.
