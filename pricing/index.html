Billing questions

Before you subscribe.

Will I be charged when the trial ends?

Yes, unless you cancel before the 7 days are up. Canceling during the trial costs nothing, and you can keep using Pro until the trial ends.

Does Pro renew automatically?

Yes. Monthly plans renew every month and yearly plans every year until you cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. On Safari, change plans in your Apple ID subscriptions. On Chrome and Edge, use the manage link in your receipt email. The change takes effect at your next renewal.

Can I get a refund?

For Safari, refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Chrome and Edge, reply to your receipt email and we'll sort it out.

I use Safari and Chrome. Do I need to pay twice?

For Pro on both, yes, because Apple and Lemon Squeezy can't see each other's purchases. Ad blocking is free on both, so most people only buy Pro where they browse the most.

What happens if I cancel?

Pro features turn off at the end of your paid period. Ad blocking keeps working for free, and your settings stay as they were in case you come back.
