Support

Help with setting up and using Blok.

Turn on Blok

iPhone and iPad: open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Blok. Turn on Allow Extension, then set All Websites to Allow.

Mac: open Safari › Settings › Extensions, check Blok, and allow it on all websites.

A site looks broken

Tap the Blok icon in Safari's toolbar and choose Pause on this site. The page reloads without Blok. Please let us know which site so we can fix it.

I still see a YouTube ad

YouTube changes often. Blok skips any ad that slips through automatically, and filter updates are delivered in the background. Make sure Blok is allowed on youtube.com and that you're on the latest version.

Something was labeled AI but isn't

Tap 👎 on the label. Blok removes it, labels less like it for you, and the vote helps improve detection for everyone. You can also lower the sensitivity in the Blok app.

Subscriptions

Blok Pro is billed through your Apple ID. To cancel or change plans, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Blok app and tap Restore purchases.

Contact

Report a problem or request a feature by opening an issue on GitHub.
