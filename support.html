Setting up and using Blok

Turn on Blok

iPhone and iPad: open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Blok. Turn on Allow Extension, then set All Websites to Allow.

Mac: open Safari › Settings › Extensions, check Blok, and allow it on all websites.

Chrome and Edge: Blok is on as soon as you install it. Click the puzzle-piece icon and pin Blok to see what it blocked on each page.

A site looks broken

Click the Blok button in the toolbar and choose Pause on this site. The page reloads without Blok. Please tell us which site so we can fix it.

I still see a YouTube ad

YouTube changes often. Blok skips any ad that gets through, and filter updates arrive in the background. Make sure Blok is allowed on youtube.com and you're on the latest version.

Something was labeled AI but isn't

Tap thumbs down on the label. Blok removes it, labels less like it for you, and your vote improves detection for everyone. You can also lower the sensitivity in Blok's settings.

Subscriptions in Safari

Blok Pro for Safari is billed through your Apple ID. To cancel or change plans, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore Pro on a new device, open the Blok app and tap Restore purchases.

License keys in Chrome and Edge

After you buy Pro, your license key arrives by email from Lemon Squeezy. Click the Blok button, choose Settings, paste the key and click Activate. One key works in up to 5 browsers, Chrome and Edge alike.

To cancel, switch between monthly and yearly, update your card or find a lost key, use the Manage subscription link in your receipt email, or look up your order at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders.
