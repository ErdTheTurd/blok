01

What it removes

Blok uses the same filter lists as uBlock Origin, more than 100,000 rules, and runs them through the browser's built-in blocker so pages load faster, not slower. Sites can't easily tell it's there.

  • Banner, sidebar and pop-up adsEasyList, uBlock
  • Trackers and analytics pixelsEasyPrivacy
  • YouTube video adsBefore and mid-roll
  • YouTube feed, search and Shorts adsAll placements
  • “Turn off your ad blocker” wallsNeutralized
  • Cookie banners and pop-upsOptional
  • Sponsor segments in videosPro · SponsorBlock

02

What it labels

Blok doesn't delete things it thinks are AI. It puts a label on them with a percentage, so you can decide for yourself. Hide blurs the post. The thumbs tell Blok when it got one right or wrong.

Where the percentage comes from

Labels the AI company added

Under the EU AI Act and similar rules, more AI tools now tag what they make. Blok reads Content Credentials (C2PA), IPTC metadata and YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure. When a tag is there, the label says so.

Analysis on your device

Most AI text isn't tagged, so Blok looks at phrasing, structure and rhythm, on your own device. Nothing you read is uploaded. On Apple devices with Apple Intelligence, Pro can run a second, deeper check.

Votes from other people

Every thumbs up or down is sent as an anonymous fingerprint of the content, never the content itself. When enough people agree, everyone's labels get more accurate.

05

Questions

How accurate are the AI labels?
When content carries an official AI tag, Blok is close to certain. Without one, it's an estimate, which is why it shows a percentage instead of hiding things. If a label is wrong, tap thumbs down and Blok removes it and adjusts to you. You can also make labels more or less sensitive.
Will websites know I'm blocking ads?
Usually not. Blok hides elements in a way pages can't inspect, and it disables the common scripts that look for ad blockers. If a site still breaks, pause Blok on that site from the toolbar button.
Is there a Windows version?
Yes. On Windows, Blok is an extension for Chrome and Edge. It blocks the same ads and shows the same labels. If you buy Pro there, you get a license key by email.
Can Blok see what I browse?
No. Blocking and AI checks happen on your device, and there are no accounts. A vote sends a one-way fingerprint of the content, the site's domain and your vote. You can read the full privacy policy.
Does it slow down my browser?
No. The browser does the blocking itself, and AI checks only run on content you scroll to. Most pages load faster because the ads never download.
How do I turn it on?
iPhone and iPad: Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Blok. Mac: Safari › Settings › Extensions. Chrome and Edge: it's on as soon as you install it. Pin it to the toolbar to see what it blocked.
