What it does

Two problems. One extension.

The web got buried under ads, and now under machine-written filler. HaltBlock clears both without slowing your browser down.

YouTube without the ads.

HaltBlock takes the ads out of a video before the player loads them, so there's nothing to skip. That covers ads before and during videos, in the home feed, in search and in Shorts.

If YouTube changes something and an ad gets through, HaltBlock mutes it and jumps to the end. With Pro, it also skips the sponsor reads inside videos.

A label on anything that reads like a chatbot.

Posts, comments, reviews and articles that look machine-written get a small label with a percentage, right where you're reading. Images and videos get one too.

Nothing is deleted. Tap Hide to blur it, or use the thumbs to tell HaltBlock when it got one wrong.

FAQ

The questions people ask first.

How accurate is the AI detection?

No detector is perfect, which is why HaltBlock shows a confidence score instead of silently deleting things. When content carries an official AI label (like Content Credentials or YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure), HaltBlock is near-certain. Otherwise it combines on-device analysis with community votes, and your own 👍 / 👎 tune it to you.

Will sites know I'm using an ad blocker?

HaltBlock is built to stay out of sight. Elements are hidden in a way pages can't inspect, and common ad-blocker detection scripts are neutralized. If a site ever breaks, pause HaltBlock for that site with one tap.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. On Windows, HaltBlock is an extension for Chrome and Edge, with the same ad blocking and AI labels. Buy Pro on this site and you'll get a license key by email that works in up to 5 browsers.
