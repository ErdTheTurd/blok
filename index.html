FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Blok work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Blok is a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac. One purchase covers all your devices, and Pro supports Family Sharing.

How accurate is the AI detection?

No detector is perfect, which is why Blok shows a confidence score instead of silently deleting things. When content carries an official AI label (like Content Credentials or YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure), Blok is near-certain. Otherwise it combines on-device analysis with community votes, and your own 👍 / 👎 tune it to you.

Will sites know I'm using an ad blocker?

Blok is built to stay out of sight. Elements are hidden in a way pages can't inspect, and common ad-blocker detection scripts are neutralized. If a site ever breaks, pause Blok for that site with one tap.

Does Blok see my browsing?

No. Ad blocking and AI checks happen on your device. Blok has no accounts and keeps no browsing history. When you vote, only an anonymous fingerprint of the content is sent, unless you choose to share samples.

Does it slow Safari down?

Network blocking is handled natively by Safari, and AI checks only run on content you actually scroll to, so pages typically load faster with Blok than without it.

How do I turn it on?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Blok, then allow it on all websites. On Mac: Safari › Settings › Extensions, then check Blok.
