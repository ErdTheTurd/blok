What it does

Two jobs. One extension.

Ad blocking is free. Pro adds AI labels and YouTube sponsor skipping.

  • 1

    Blocks ads and trackers in Safari

    Banner ads, pop-ups, trackers and YouTube ads are removed before they load — on Mac (Safari) and Edge.

  • 2

    Cleans up YouTube

    Ads before and during videos, plus feed, search and Shorts ads. Pro also skips sponsor reads, self-promo and subscribe reminders via SponsorBlock (turn off in settings).

  • 3

    Labels AI-generated content

    Text, images and YouTube videos get a small on-page label when they look machine-made. Hide blurs the content without deleting it.
