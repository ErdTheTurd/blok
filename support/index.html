Help with Blok

Setting up, fixing problems and managing Pro

Turn on Blok

iPhone and iPad: open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Blok. Turn on Allow Extension, then set All Websites to Allow. Open the Blok app once so it can set up.

Mac: open Safari › Settings › Extensions, check Blok, and allow it on all websites. If Blok isn't listed, open the Blok app once, then check again.

Chrome and Edge: Blok is on as soon as you install it. Click the puzzle-piece icon and pin Blok to see what it blocked on each page.

Blok isn't blocking anything

  • Check that Blok is allowed on all websites, not just when you click it. In Safari this is the "All Websites" setting above.
  • Click the Blok button and make sure the site isn't paused. Paused sites show Resume on this site.
  • Make sure Ad blocking is turned on in the popup.
  • Reload the page. Pages that were already open when you installed Blok need one reload.

A site looks broken

Click the Blok button in the toolbar and choose Pause on this site. The page reloads without Blok, and every other site stays protected. Please tell us which site so we can fix it.

I still see a YouTube ad

YouTube changes often. Blok skips any ad that gets through, and filter updates arrive in the background. Make sure Blok is allowed on youtube.com and you're on the latest version. If ads keep showing on one video, open an issue with the video link.

I don't see any AI labels

  • AI labels are part of Pro. Check that Pro is active in the Blok app (Safari) or Blok settings (Chrome and Edge).
  • Check that AI text, image and video labels are turned on in the popup.
  • Try raising sensitivity to High. On Medium, Blok only labels content it's at least 70% sure about.
  • Short text isn't checked. Blok needs a few sentences to judge writing.

Something was labeled AI but isn't

Tap thumbs down on the label. Blok removes it, labels less like it for you, and your vote improves detection for everyone. You can also lower the sensitivity in Blok's settings.

Subscriptions in Safari

Blok Pro for Safari is billed through your Apple ID. To cancel or change plans, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore Pro on a new device, open the Blok app and tap Restore purchases. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

License keys in Chrome and Edge

After you buy Pro, your license key arrives by email from Lemon Squeezy. Click the Blok button, choose Settings, paste the key and click Activate. One key works in up to 5 browsers, Chrome and Edge alike.

To cancel, switch between monthly and yearly, update your card or find a lost key, use the Manage subscription link in your receipt email, or look up your order at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders.

If activation says the key is already in use in 5 browsers, remove an old browser from the manage page in your receipt email, then try again.

Uninstall Blok

iPhone and iPad: delete the Blok app. Mac: drag Blok from Applications to the Trash. Chrome and Edge: right-click the Blok button and choose Remove. Uninstalling doesn't cancel Pro, so cancel your subscription first if you no longer want it.

Contact

Report a problem or request a feature by opening an issue on GitHub. Include your browser, device and the site you were on.
