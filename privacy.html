Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Blok is built so that it doesn't need to know who you are or what you browse. This policy explains the small amount of data Blok handles and why.

What stays on your device

  • Browsing. Ad and tracker blocking is performed by Safari on your device. Blok never sends your browsing history or page addresses to us.
  • AI checks. Text and image analysis runs on your device, including optional Apple Intelligence checks.
  • Settings and stats. Your settings, paused sites and blocked-item counts are stored locally and shared only between the Blok app and its Safari extension.

What is sent when you vote

When you tap 👍 or 👎 on a label, Blok sends:

  • a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the content, which cannot be turned back into the content itself,
  • the content type (text, image or video), the website's domain, your vote, and Blok's score,
  • a random device identifier that is not linked to your Apple ID, name or email.

To show community scores, Blok also looks up content fingerprints. These lookups contain fingerprints only.

Optional sample sharing

If you turn on "Share flagged samples to improve Blok" (off by default), the text excerpt or image address you voted on is included with your vote and used to improve Blok's detection models. You can turn this off at any time in the Blok app.

Third-party services

  • Supabase stores anonymous votes and community scores.
  • SponsorBlock (Pro, optional) is queried with the YouTube video ID you're watching to find sponsor segments.
  • Apple processes subscriptions through the App Store. Blok never sees your payment details.

What Blok never does

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No reading of form fields, passwords or private messages.

Waitlist

If you join the launch waitlist on this website, we store your email address only to tell you when Blok is available, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

Blok does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub or visit the support page.
