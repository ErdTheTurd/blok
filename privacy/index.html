Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Blok is built so that it doesn't need to know who you are or what you browse. This page explains the small amount of data Blok handles and why. It applies to Blok for Safari and Blok for Chrome and Edge.

What stays on your device

  • Browsing. Ads and trackers are blocked by your browser, on your device. Blok never sends your browsing history or the addresses of pages you visit.
  • AI checks. Text and image analysis runs on your device, including the optional Apple Intelligence check in Safari.
  • Settings and counts. Your settings, paused sites and blocked-item counts are stored locally. In Safari they're shared only between the Blok app and its extension.

What is sent when you vote

When you tap thumbs up or thumbs down on a label, Blok sends:

  • a one-way fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of the content, which can't be turned back into the content,
  • the content type (text, image or video), the website's domain, your vote and Blok's score,
  • a random device identifier that isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID or Google account.

To show community scores, Blok also looks up content fingerprints. These lookups contain fingerprints only.

Optional sample sharing

If you turn on “Share flagged samples to improve Blok” (off by default), the text excerpt or image address you voted on is included with your vote and used to improve detection. You can turn it off at any time in Blok's settings.

License keys (Chrome and Edge)

Pro for Chrome and Edge is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the reseller: it takes the payment, handles sales tax, and emails you a license key. To confirm the key is active, the extension sends Lemon Squeezy the key, a per-browser activation ID, and the browser name (Chrome or Edge). It never sends the sites you visit.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores anonymous votes, community scores and the waitlist.
  • Lemon Squeezy handles Chrome and Edge purchases and license keys. See their privacy policy.
  • SponsorBlock (Pro, optional) receives the ID of the YouTube video you're watching, to find sponsor segments.
  • Apple handles Safari subscriptions through the App Store. Blok never sees your payment details.

What Blok never does

  • No ads, no analytics tools, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No reading of form fields, passwords or private messages.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, we store your email address and the browser you picked, only to tell you when Blok is available. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Children

Blok doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
